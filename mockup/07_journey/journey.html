Journey

Builds your first real plan step by step, while you keep tapping.

  • Stage 1 · rough draft: a first guess of your week.
  • Stage 2 · five question steps: work split, family, hobbies, stuck time, chores.
  • Stage 3 · generated template: a day template made from your answers, with undo.
  • A trial frame of about 30 days ("probably longer than 30 days").

Planned for app version 1.1. First sketch: mockup v5, tab "Journey" (on Drive only, not online).
