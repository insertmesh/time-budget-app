How the app moves (v1)

  1. Bottom bar, 5 buttons: Tap · Plans · Overviews · Rewards · More. Same place on every main screen, always with labels (icons alone are guesswork).
  2. Tap is home. The app always opens on Tap. The Tap button shows the running tile (a mini Work patch while Work runs), so the bar also tells you what is running.
  3. The timer never stops when you leave Tap. On every other screen a thin strip above the bar shows "Work · 1:23:05 ›". Tapping it goes back to Tap.
  4. Inside a tab, screens stack. Opening something inside a tab (e.g. a goal stamp in Overviews) slides the new screen in with "‹ Overviews" at the top left. The bar stays.
  5. Each tab remembers where you were (Plans stays on Week, Overviews on Month). Tapping the button of the tab you are already on goes back to that tab's first screen.
  6. Back (‹ or the phone's back / edge swipe): one screen up inside the tab → on a tab's first screen: back to Tap → on Tap: leaves the app (timer keeps running).
  7. No swiping between tabs. Sideways swipes would fire by accident while you tap tiles. Only the back edge swipe works.
  8. Page tabs move to the top. Day / Week / Month (Plans, Overviews) and Checkout / Progress / Rewards (Rewards) become a switch under the title, so there is only ever one bar at the bottom.
  9. Leaving Plans with unsaved changes (any bar button, back, or the strip) asks first: Save · Review · Revert (Decision 2026-11-13). Try it: Plans → "+15 min Hobby" → tap another button.
  10. Evening check-in (3A): at checkout time it opens by itself over everything (the bar is covered). "Later ✕" closes it; the Tap button keeps a red number badge (open times), and Tap shows "3 times need you · sort now". Sorting the last one removes the badge.
  11. Full-screen moments cover the bar: evening checkout, morning check-in, "Nighty night!", onboarding / tour. They end by finishing or closing, never by a tab tap.
