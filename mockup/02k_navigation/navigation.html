New in v2

The screens in the phone are now the real designed mockups (Tap 02g, Plans 02a, Overviews 02c, Rewards 02d, Categories / Goals 02b, Settings 02e, Rules + tour 02f), not stand-ins. Inside the phone their review controls and notes are hidden. To see those, open a page on its own from the review bar at the top. Links inside a screen go through the app navigation: e.g. Tap → "Adjust in Plans" switches to the Plans tab, an Overviews goal stamp opens Goals inside Overviews.

How the app moves

  1. Bottom bar, 5 buttons: Tap · Plans · Overviews · Rewards · More. Same place on every main screen, always with labels (icons alone are guesswork).
  2. Tap is home. The app always opens on Tap. The Tap button can show the running tile (a mini Work patch while Work runs), so the bar also tells you what is running.
  3. The timer never stops when you leave Tap. On every other screen a thin strip above the bar shows the running activity and its clock. Tapping it goes back to Tap.
  4. Inside a tab, screens stack. Opening something inside a tab (e.g. a goal stamp in Overviews) shows the new screen with "‹ Overviews" at the top left. The bar stays.
  5. Each tab remembers where you were (Plans stays on Week, Overviews on Month). Tapping the button of the tab you are already on goes back to that tab's first screen.
  6. Back (‹ or the phone's back / edge swipe): one screen up inside the tab → on a tab's first screen: back to Tap → on Tap: leaves the app (timer keeps running).
  7. No swiping between tabs. Sideways swipes would fire by accident while you tap tiles. Only the back edge swipe works.
  8. Page tabs move to the top. Rewards' Checkout / Progress / Rewards sit at the top inside the app (done here already), so there is only ever one bar at the bottom.
  9. Leaving Plans with unsaved changes asks first: Save · Review · Revert (Decision 2026-11-13, designed in Plans v7).
  10. Evening check-in (3A): at checkout time it opens by itself over everything (the bar is hidden while it is open). Closing it with times still open leaves a red number badge on the Tap button and a "… need you · sort now" button on the tap screen. Try: "Evening checkout" → close it → look at the bar.
  11. Full-screen moments cover the bar: evening checkout, morning check-in, "Nighty night!", onboarding / tour. They end by finishing or closing, never by a tab tap.
