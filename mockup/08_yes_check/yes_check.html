Yes-check & Foster / Reduce

Shows what a new commitment costs before you say yes.

  • Yes-check: before you agree to something, see what it pushes out, in this order: unnamed time, then the Games cap, then flexible items, then fixed items (warning only). Accept applies it for real.
  • Foster / Reduce: pick one thing to grow and one to shrink, set the amount with a slider, see the change, apply, undo.
  • Discovery list: "I just remembered something": park a new activity and sort it into a group later.

Planned for app version 1.1. First sketch: mockup v5, tab "Plan" (on Drive only, not online).
