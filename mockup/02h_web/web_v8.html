FAQ

Do I have to plan?

No. Planning is always skippable, and you can use the app with taps only. Pick "Quick start" on the Start here page and you never see a plan.

What if I forget to tap?

Then the last tile kept running. The gap is data, not a failure. At night the app asks about the unclear bits, and "Sorry, forgot to tap" and "TBH? No idea" are both fine answers.

Where does my data go?

Version 1 saves everything in your browser. No account, nothing is sent anywhere. Export a backup file any time. What is it for? Looking back at where your hours went, and suggestions for changes. Suggestions you can ignore.

What if I try it and hate it?

Close it. Export your data first if you want to keep it. Nothing else happens, nobody is notified, there are no streaks to lose.
