FAQ

The basics

Do I need an account?

No. Version 1 has no login and no account. You open it and tap.

What does it cost?
Not decided yet. Pricing is an open question for the project, so the page says nothing about it.
Which devices does it work on?

Version 1 works in your browser, on a Windows PC. Phone and other devices come later, with no date promised.

Is it like a money budget?

The idea is the same: see where it went, then decide where it should go. The difference is that you can't save time for later, only spend it. Nobody grades the result.

Using it

Do I have to plan?

No. Planning is always skippable, and you can use the app with taps only. Pick "Quick start" on the Start here page and you never see a plan.

What if I forget to tap?

Then the last tile kept running. The gap is data, not a failure. At night the app asks about the unclear bits, and "Sorry, forgot to tap" and "TBH? No idea" are both fine answers.

Why is there no stop button?

Time doesn't stop, so the clock doesn't either. You switch to the next thing. Sleep has its own tile, so it's one tap before you close the app.

Will it nag me?

No alarms and no scolding. Plan prompts and suggestions are offers you can ignore. Sounds are not part of version 1.

Do I have to use all of it?

No. One tap on a tile is already the whole app. The rest unlocks when you feel like it.

Can I turn off the points and comments?

That's the plan: a plain mode with data only, no points, no little comments. Rewards are earned by being thorough, never bought, and points only go up.

Does it work with my calendar?

Optional calendar sync is part of the design: import your dates, resync when new ones come. It isn't built yet.

Data and trust

Where does my data go?

Version 1 saves everything in your browser. No account, nothing is sent anywhere. Export a backup file any time. What is it for? Looking back at where your hours went, and suggestions for changes. Suggestions you can ignore.

What if I try it and hate it?

Close it. Export your data first if you want to keep it. Nothing else happens, nobody is notified, there are no streaks to lose.

Brain stuff

Is this made for ADHD or autistic people?

It's made with brains that do time differently in mind, and it doesn't ask for a diagnosis. Plenty of people lose hours without noticing. It's not a treatment and not medical advice.

Does it work for everyone?

We don't know. Nobody has studied time-logging apps for ADHD or autistic adults yet. That's what trying it is for. More in "Why though?".
