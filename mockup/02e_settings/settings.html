New in settings v2

  • Alarms & notifications (new section, chip "Alarms"): one main switch; 4 lines (Start the day, Evening checkout, Next planned activity, Bedtime), each on/off + Banner / Sound / Alarm and "▶ test" (a fake phone message drops in at the top). Alarm sound: 3 built-in sounds or your own mp3. Vibrate when sound is off, with a pattern.
  • Known limit (fairly sure): the app is a web page for now. A web page can show notifications, but it cannot reliably ring a real alarm or play your own mp3 while it is closed, and iPhones do not let web pages vibrate. Full alarms need the Android app later (09_later). The settings are designed now so the app can grow into them.
