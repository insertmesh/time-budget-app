The rules
(there are only a few)

Time is always running, whether you plan for it or not.

It's just data and we make it visible. We can make it more fun to play with too, but in the end, what you do with it is your choice.

What's it for? Looking back at where your hours went, and suggestions for changes. Suggestions you can ignore.

1
Your time needs purpose.

Rest counts. Fun counts. Procrastination doesn't, and neither does overtime nobody chose. Happened anyway? Name it. "Stuck" is a fine name.

2
Your time is limited.

Don't know where it goes? Then you're not in charge of it.

3
Time is relative.

You can't always decide what to do with it. But with no plan, there's nothing to adjust.

4
Spend it more consciously.

The goal isn't doing more. It's doing less of what you don't value.

Didn't fix your plans for the hobby day? Your bad. Learn to fix days for yourself :)

Reachable later from Settings → "Rules" and from the last onboarding page.
