New in v3: mini game "Guess your week"

  • When: once a week, after the last checkout of the week (Sunday). A card on Progress says "Guess before you look!". Also in Rewards > Mini games > Play demo.
  • Play: 3 sliders: guess your hours this week for 3 categories (one you want to reduce, one you want to grow, one random). Steps of 30 min, no hints. "Just show me" skips the game (no points, no shame).
  • Reveal: per category the real hours (bar in category colour), your guess (▼ marker) and your plan (dashed line). Points: within 1 h = +3 "Spot on!", within 3 h = +2 "Close!", further = +1 "Time flies, huh?". Every guess earns something, never minus. Max +9.
  • Time sense track: how far off your guesses were, week by week. Getting closer = your sense of time is getting better. That is the real reward behind the game.

  • One general layout, five skins. Pages, numbers and rules are the same for every theme. The skin only changes the points name + icon, how a day looks in the collection, and the confetti. Switch "Skin" above, or unlock a theme and press "Use".

  • All 5 FX animated with the approved timings. Do a full checkout to see 1 → 2 → 3 → 5 in a row; unlock a theme for 4. "Replay FX" plays each one alone.
  • Real-life treats (Rewards tab, top). When you use less time than planned on things you want to reduce (Work overtime, Stuck), that time is "freed". Once enough is freed, the app makes a treat slot in your plan. You fill it with one of your own treats (price in points), or leave it as free time. "Guilt-free gaming" = the app-credits idea as a self-made rule: shown, not enforced.
  • Mini games: still concept cards. Pick which one to mock first.
  • Wednesday is now a checked-out day, so tonight's checkout completes the week (to show FX 3). Rest days still show in the month collection.
