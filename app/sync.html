Sync

Keeps your taps the same on your phone, PC and the web app. Your data goes into a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can open that folder; it cannot see your other Drive files, and nobody else (also not the app maker) gets a copy.

…

Off = a "Resume" button here instead. Your taps are never lost while sync is paused: they wait on this device and catch up.

Disconnect keeps your data on this device and in Drive; it only stops syncing here.

Phone + PC: connect on each device with the same Google account. A tap on any device counts as a switch: when two devices each run a tile, the later tap wins.
